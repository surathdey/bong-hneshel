## Section F · Integrations (optional, we'll help with each)

Tick the integrations you want at launch. Each requires an account +
API key from the linked provider.

- ☐ **Google Maps** — required for delivery address autocomplete + live
  driver tracking. We'll help you generate a key.
- ☐ **Twilio SMS** — order status texts to customers. Requires a Twilio
  account + a provisioned phone number.
- ☐ **Emergent Email (Resend)** — order receipts by email. Managed by us;
  no signup needed, but we may need you to verify a sending domain (SPF
  / DKIM DNS records).
- ☐ **Push notifications** — order updates on the customer's phone.
  Requires a Firebase project + `google-services.json`. Only works on
  real device builds (not Expo Go / web preview).
- ☐ **Google Sign-In** — one-tap customer login. Managed by us via
  Emergent Auth.

## Section I · What happens next

Once we receive this form:

1. **Day 1** — we create your GitHub fork, Emergent project, and Super
   Admin tenant record. You'll receive:
   - A **preview URL** to test the app on a phone browser
   - Your **Admin login credentials** (Admin portal at `/admin`)
   - A short **welcome video** walking you through the Admin portal
2. **Day 2–3** — we finalise branding, add your menu, and hand you the
   Admin portal so you can add / edit items.
3. **Day 4** — we submit your app to TestFlight (iOS) and internal
   testing (Android). You install both on your phone and approve.
4. **Day 5–14** — we submit to the App Store and Play Store. Approval
   takes 1–7 days; we handle any rejection cycles.
5. **Launch day** — your app goes live on both stores. We stay on-call
   for 48 hours to catch anything unusual.

Questions? Email us at **onboarding@suparnadey.com** — reply within
one business day.
