You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

3. How we use your data

  • Prepare, package and deliver your food orders.
  • Communicate order confirmations, delays, and delivery arrivals via email/SMS/push.
  • Award and redeem loyalty points and coupons.
  • Provide customer support and process refunds.
  • Comply with tax, food-safety and other legal obligations.

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Who we share data with

  • Stripe — processes card payments. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Google — provides Maps & routing for delivery. Google Privacy Policy
  • Delivery couriers — receive your first name, phone, and delivery address only for the active order.
  • Government / courts — only when compelled by valid legal process.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Active account: until you request deletion.
  • Order records: 7 years (Canadian tax law).
  • Deleted accounts: profile data is removed immediately; order records are anonymised (no name, email, phone or address remains).

6. Your rights

Under Canadian PIPEDA and Ontario privacy law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate.
  • Delete your account — do this in-app at Profile → Delete my account, or email privacy@curryandco.ca.
  • Object to processing, or withdraw consent (e.g. turn off push notifications).
  • Complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you feel we've mishandled your data.

7. Children

Curry & Co. is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

8. Cookies & tracking

Our website uses minimal first-party cookies for session management. The mobile app uses secure device storage for your session token — no advertising trackers, no cross-app identifiers.

9. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Payment cards never touch our servers — Stripe handles them under PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll notify you via email or in-app. Continued use of Curry & Co. after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Terms of Service

Ordering

Our weekly menu changes every Sunday. Ordering days and delivery days are shown in the app and may change without notice. Prices are in Canadian dollars and include applicable taxes.

Payment

Card payments are processed through Stripe. Cash on delivery is available in some zones — the exact cash amount must be ready. Interac e-Transfer instructions are shown at checkout when selected.

Delivery

We deliver within the Greater Toronto Area. Missed deliveries where the customer is unreachable are re-attempted once, after which the order is forfeited. Refunds and re-deliveries are handled case-by-case at our discretion.

Allergens

All food is prepared in a facility that also processes dairy, gluten, nuts, seafood and eggs. Please review allergen tags before ordering. We cannot guarantee zero cross-contamination.

Cancellations & refunds

Orders may be cancelled for a full refund up to 4 hours before the delivery window. After that, the order enters preparation and is non-refundable except at our discretion for quality issues.

Account termination

We may suspend or terminate accounts for fraud, chargebacks, abuse of our couriers, or repeated no-shows. You may terminate your own account at any time via Profile → Delete my account.
